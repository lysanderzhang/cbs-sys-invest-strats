## Conventions needed to interpret the files

- CSV is the primary source; XLSX is a precision sensitivity. Raw inputs are never changed.
- Spot levels are normalized to USD per foreign unit; all backtest returns are recomputed arithmetic returns.
- Annual quoted percentages are divided by 100 and modeled as continuous financing proxies on ACT/365; weekend intervals accrue actual calendar days.
- Weights, rates and risk exposure chosen at close t earn the next observation's return. Long/short initial USD notionals are +1/−1. Full data timing is assumed at a common fixing.
- The exact funding-adjusted currency return is `exp(y_foreign*dt)*(X_next/X_now) - exp(y_USD*dt)`. USD terms cancel in the net-zero portfolio; do not subtract USD interest again in Sharpe.
- Both portfolio sides are normalized separately. Ties use alphabetical order; spread allocation uses distance from the nine-yield median and equal-side fallback for a zero spread sum.
- Thresholds use the preceding 252 as-of observations, exclude the current value, and trade next-period returns. Missing VIX is forward-filled only after its first observation, never backwards. Historical risk is 21 arithmetic AUDJPY returns, sample sd×sqrt(252).
- Annualized arithmetic mean and volatility use 252; geometric growth compounds daily arithmetic returns. VaR, ES and drawdown are positive losses; worst daily return is signed. Wealth excludes risk-free collateral interest.
- Baselines use 2,980 dates; six-filter comparisons use 2,727; historical comparisons use 2,707. Warm-up missingness is not replaced with zero.
- CSV returns/risk values are decimals; source yields/IV/VIX are percentage points. Historical RV is decimal annualized volatility. Report tables explicitly show percent units and five decimal places.
- The fixed 20-day/2,000-draw block intervals condition on selected realized series and are not selection-adjusted. Exposure/volatility controls use ex-post scales and are diagnostic benchmarks.

All paths resolve from the project root; original OneDrive locations are provenance
only. Numerical CSVs use 12 significant digits, with internal double precision.
Historical validation logs are retained as records; the current review status is
`results/audit/verification_summary.json` and `report/AUDIT.md`.
