1)  (Investor pitch) In this assignment, you are to create an investor pitch (presentation) for a
systematic  investment  strategy.  The  pitch  can  be  for  raising  assets  (to  manage  with  the
investment strategy), working capital (to help establish a firm that will employ the investment
strategy), or both. Please identify clearly the objective of your presentation.

In your presentation, you can use, if you feel it is necessary, return data, either fictitious or
real. If you use fictitious data, you have to synthesize the data appropriately. If you use real
data, you need to explain how the data come about.

In your presentation, you must:
•  Provide  context  for  the  landscape  of  your  strategy  (available  strategies  in  the  same

category, commonalities/differences, etc.).

•  Explain the main idea, along with sufficient technical details, of your strategy. “Technical
details”  refers  to  both  methodological  details  and  characteristics  of  the  investment
results.

•  Establish the “innovation” (if any) of your strategy.
•  Demonstrate the competitive advantage(s) of your strategy.
•  Address the robustness of any evidence you are presenting/using.
•  Discuss the feasibility of your strategy in relation to the goal of your pitch (transaction

costs, liquidity, capacity, target capital if your venture is a start-up, etc.).

•  Anticipate, and present answers to, the questions your audience may have about your

pitch.

Note:    This  is  an  open-ended,  perhaps  even  “vague”  assignment  that  may  appear  as  “too
advanced.” This is intentional: the goal is to invite you to be creative about your own context
for a systematic investment strategy for which you have the ultimate responsibility.
